Promotion gates · Before capture

Every record must clear five gates before it becomes a client pursuit.

This keeps a broad research sweep from turning stale dates, weak search terms, or adjacent technology buys into false pipeline.

1 · Status

Open the official record. Confirm it is active or identify the successor-research path.

2 · Fit

Map the requirement to supplier onboarding, payment integrity, fraud prevention, third-party risk, or an approved adjacent capability.

3 · Buyer

Identify the requirement owner, acquisition office, published contact, and account context.

4 · Access

Confirm ApexAnalytix can prime; otherwise validate the eligible paper holder and a real teaming route.

5 · Action

Name the next irreversible decision: respond, request package, qualify partner, pursue successor, or pass.
